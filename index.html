Welcome to Ventanas

The pinnacle of contemporary living, in the heart of Westlake.

Nestled within the prestigious landscape of Westlake, Texas, Ventanas is an exclusive neighborhood that represents the pinnacle of contemporary living.

Each residence showcases striking modern architecture — clean lines, expansive glass facades, and innovative design that seamlessly blends indoor and outdoor space. Residents enjoy privileged access to everything Entrada has to offer, from resort-style amenities to exclusive dining and recreation, while Ventanas holds onto the intimate, boutique feel of a private gated enclave.

Miles of scenic walking trails wind through preserved green space and connect to Westlake's broader trail network — peaceful retreats for morning runs, evening strolls, and family bike rides. It is the perfect balance of contemporary luxury, premium amenities, and natural beauty.
